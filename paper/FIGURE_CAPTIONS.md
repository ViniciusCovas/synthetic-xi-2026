# Figure Captions

## Figure 1. Replication consistency of the primary championship estimand

Synthetic XI champion probability in the 10,000-run confirmatory experiment, the independently seeded 50,000-run precision replication, and the secondary pooled 60,000-run summary. Points indicate estimates and horizontal lines indicate 95% Wilson intervals. The 10,000-run estimate remains the preregistered primary confirmatory result.

## Figure 2. H5 variance differences

Synthetic-minus-Real differences in goals, expected-goals, and shot variance in the independently seeded 50,000-run replication. Negative estimates indicate lower outcome variability for the Synthetic XI. Horizontal lines indicate 95% bootstrap intervals. Scales differ across the three estimands and are displayed separately.

## Figure 3. H6 extreme-outcome probability differences

Synthetic-minus-Real differences in the probability of scoring at least three goals, exceeding the preregistered xG threshold, exceeding the shot threshold, and winning by the preregistered margin. Negative values indicate a higher frequency of extreme outcomes for the Real Best XI. Horizontal lines indicate 95% bootstrap intervals.

## Figure 4. Monte Carlo precision versus structural uncertainty

Synthetic XI champion probability under the frozen confirmatory specification, the independent precision replication, and the nested model-uncertainty analysis. The first two intervals are 95% Wilson intervals under match-level randomness. The wider nested interval is the 2.5th–97.5th percentile across 160 parameter worlds and should not be interpreted as a conventional sampling confidence interval.

## Figure 5. H7 context-dependent xG-rate differences

Synthetic-minus-Real expected-goals event-rate differences across score state, numerical state, and match phase in the 50,000-run replication. Cell opacity represents absolute effect magnitude. Positive values favor the Synthetic XI; negative values favor the Real Best XI. The largest pattern is lower Synthetic XI production in tied equal-strength states and higher production when trailing at equal strength in the second half.
